The Creation of the Movement of the Non-Larpers

The Movement of the Non-Larpers (MNL) was created by Goat_420 (AmundMN) after becoming frustrated with people taking fictional roleplay far too seriously.

Inspired by revolutionary leaders such as Lenin, AmundMN wrote the Non-Larper Manifesto, calling for people to stop confusing fictional worlds with reality.

He quickly became the movement's Le Leader, with supporters gathering around his revolutionary message:

“Wise man never larps, We will overthrow the larpers!”

— Amund
